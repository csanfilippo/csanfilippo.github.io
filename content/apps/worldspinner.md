**WorldSpinner** is a fun app that helps you discover new places to visit — without leaving your couch.

### Shake to discover

Just shake your phone, and it instantly suggests a random destination to spark your next trip idea. The list of locations updates regularly, so there's always something new to explore.

### Compatibility

iPhone, iPod touch, Mac (Apple Silicon), and Apple Vision. Available in English and Italian. Lightweight at 11.3 MB.

**No longer available on the App Store.**
