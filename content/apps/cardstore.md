# CardStore

**CardStore** is a free iOS app that keeps all your loyalty, membership, and store cards in one place — on your phone, always with you.

Adding a card takes seconds: scan the barcode with your camera, give it a name, pick a color, and you're done. No accounts, no syncing, no setup. At the checkout, open the app, tap the card, and show the barcode to the cashier — screen brightness boosts automatically so it reads on the first try.

CardStore supports Italian and English, and stores everything locally on your device. No registration required.

This version is a complete rewrite of the original app, built with SwiftUI and the latest Swift technology. If you were using the previous version, your cards are migrated automatically.
