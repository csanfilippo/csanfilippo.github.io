# CardStore

**CardStore** is a free iOS app that keeps all your loyalty, membership, and store cards in one place — on your phone, always with you.

# Screenshots

![CardStoreScreen](/images/cardstore_screen.png)

### Add a card

Scan the barcode with your camera, give it a name, pick a color — no accounts, no registration.

### Use it at checkout

Open the app, tap the card, and show the barcode to the cashier. Screen brightness boosts automatically so it reads on the first try.

### Your data

Built from the ground up in SwiftUI with the latest Swift, CardStore keeps everything local to your device — nothing syncs, nothing leaves your phone. Moving from the previous version? Your cards migrate automatically.

### Compatibility

iPhone, iOS 18.5 or later. Available in Italian and English.
