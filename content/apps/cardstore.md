# CardStore

**CardStore** is a free iOS app that keeps all your loyalty, membership, and store cards in one place — on your phone, always with you.

Adding a card takes seconds: scan the barcode with your camera, give it a name, pick a color, and you're done. No accounts, no registration. At the checkout, open the app, tap the card, and show the barcode to the cashier — screen brightness boosts automatically so it reads on the first try.

Built from the ground up in SwiftUI with the latest Swift, CardStore is fast, native, and keeps everything local to your device — nothing syncs, nothing leaves your phone. If you're moving from the previous version, your cards migrate automatically.

CardStore is compatible with iPhone, requiring iOS 18.5 or later, and supports Italian and English.
