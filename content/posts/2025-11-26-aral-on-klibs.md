Starting from yesterday, [aral](/libs/aral) is indexed on [Klibs.io](https://klibs.io/).

The project is live since [October](/posts/aral-is-here/), but due to [some bug](https://github.com/JetBrains/klibs-io-issue-management/issues/149) in Klibs.io indexing, the library wasn't showing up in search results.

They finally fixed the issue, and now aral is discoverable on Klibs.io.

Happy coding!
